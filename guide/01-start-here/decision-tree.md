# Decision tree: "My dog does X"

Start at the top. Answer each question and follow the arrow to the right page. Two minutes.

## Step 0, always

**Has a vet checked the dog since this started?**
- No → [First rule: see the vet](../03-problems/first-rule-see-the-vet.md), then come back.
- Yes → continue.

**Has the dog bitten a person or animal and broken skin, or are there children in the home with any growling, snapping, or guarding?**
- Yes → [Aggression](../03-problems/aggression.md) and [When to get help, and who to call](when-to-get-help.md). Do not self-manage.
- No → continue.

## On walks

**Barks, lunges, or growls at dogs or people on leash?**
- Body stiff, trying to get away, or hackles up → fear-based: [Leash reactivity (barking and lunging at dogs or people)](../03-problems/leash-reactivity.md)
- Whining, wagging, pulling toward the thing → frustrated greeter: same page, read the "frustration" notes
- Only when the leash is on, fine off-leash → leash frustration: same page plus [Pulling on the leash](../03-problems/pulling-on-leash.md)

**Pulls hard but is friendly?** → [Pulling on the leash](../03-problems/pulling-on-leash.md)

**Will not come back when called?** → [Recall (coming when called)](../03-problems/recall.md)

**Scared of traffic, bikes, noises outside?** → [Fear, anxiety, and noise phobia](../03-problems/fear-and-noise-phobia.md)

## At home

**Destroys things or soils the house, but only when you are out?**
- Starts within minutes of leaving, aimed at doors and windows, drooling or howling on video → [Separation anxiety](../03-problems/separation-anxiety.md)
- Random items, hours into the absence, happy on video otherwise → [Destructive chewing](../03-problems/destructive-chewing.md) (boredom) or [House training](../03-problems/house-training.md)

**Barks a lot?**
- At the window or door → alert barking: [Excessive barking](../03-problems/barking.md)
- At you, for things → demand barking: same page
- Alone in the yard → boredom: same page

**Stiffens, growls, or snaps over food, toys, beds, or a person?** → [Resource guarding](../03-problems/resource-guarding.md)

**Cannot settle, jumps, mouths, spins, zooms, especially in the evening?**
- Under 6 months → [Puppy mouthing and biting](../03-problems/puppy-mouthing.md)
- Older → [Impulse control and over-arousal](../03-problems/impulse-control-and-overarousal.md)

**Jumps on people?** → [Jumping up](../03-problems/jumping.md)

**Accidents in the house?**
- Puppy → [House training](../03-problems/house-training.md)
- Adult that used to be reliable → vet first (bladder), then [House training](../03-problems/house-training.md)
- Older dog with other changes (confusion, night waking) → [Senior dogs](../05-development/senior-dogs.md)

**Hates the crate?** → [Crate training](../03-problems/crate-training.md). If the dog panics or injures itself, [Separation anxiety](../03-problems/separation-anxiety.md).

## Handling and care

**Fights nail trims, grooming, the vet, being touched?** → [Cooperative care: vet visits, nail trims, grooming](../03-problems/cooperative-care.md)

**Terrified of fireworks or storms?** → [Fear, anxiety, and noise phobia](../03-problems/fear-and-noise-phobia.md)

**Scared of visitors or strangers in the home?** → [Treat and Retreat](../04-protocols/treat-and-retreat.md) and [Fear, anxiety, and noise phobia](../03-problems/fear-and-noise-phobia.md)

## Life stage

- New puppy → [Puppies and socialization (about 8 weeks to 6 months)](../05-development/puppies-and-socialization.md)
- 6 to 18 months and suddenly ignoring you → [Adolescence (about 6 to 18 months)](../05-development/adolescence.md)
- Just adopted an adult → [Adopted adult dogs](../05-development/adopted-adult-dogs.md)
- Older dog changing → [Senior dogs](../05-development/senior-dogs.md)

## Nothing fits

Describe the behavior in one sentence (what, where, when, with whom), video it, and take it to a certified professional. [When to get help, and who to call](when-to-get-help.md).

Evidence: routing only; see each destination page.
