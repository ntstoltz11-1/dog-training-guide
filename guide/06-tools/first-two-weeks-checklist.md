# First two weeks with a new dog: checklist

Print this. Tick as you go. It works for a puppy or an adopted adult; the age-specific pages are linked at the bottom.

**Evidence:** Consensus. This is a schedule built from the guide's other pages.

## Before the dog arrives

- [ ] Vet appointment booked for the first week
- [ ] Household plan filled in: cue words, house rules, who does what (`household-plan.md`)
- [ ] Gates or pen set up; one quiet "safe space" with a bed
- [ ] Harness, 6 foot leash, long line, ID tag, treat pouch, soft treats (`guide/01-start-here/equipment.md`)
- [ ] Enzymatic cleaner bought
- [ ] Chews and two or three food toys
- [ ] Shoes, cords, medications, and anything chewable moved out of reach
- [ ] Everyone has read `guide/01-start-here/body-language-and-threshold.md`

## Day 1 to 3: quiet and predictable

- [ ] Straight to the toilet spot on arrival; reward there
- [ ] Short tour of one or two rooms, not the whole house
- [ ] Meals, toilet trips, and bedtime at set times, written down
- [ ] No visitors, no outings beyond short leash walks nearby
- [ ] Leash or long line on in the yard; do not test the fence yet
- [ ] Start the training log (`training-log.md`): sleep, eating, accidents, how the dog is settling
- [ ] Charge the marker word: "yes" then treat, 10 times, twice a day (`guide/04-protocols/marker-training-basics.md`)
- [ ] Let the dog sleep. A lot.

## Day 4 to 7: foundations

- [ ] Vet visit, with video of anything that worries you
- [ ] Two-minute sessions, two or three times a day: name, eye contact, sit, hand touch
- [ ] Crate or safe-space conditioning started, door open, treats inside (`guide/03-problems/crate-training.md`)
- [ ] First very short alone-time practice, with a camera (`guide/03-problems/separation-anxiety.md` if there is any panic)
- [ ] One calm new thing a day: a new street, a new surface, a parked car, a person at a distance
- [ ] Handling practice: touch a paw, treat; touch an ear, treat (`guide/03-problems/cooperative-care.md`)
- [ ] Still no off-leash time, still no free run of the house

## Week 2: build

- [ ] Recall game started on a long line (`guide/03-problems/recall.md`)
- [ ] Loose-leash practice on short training walks (`guide/03-problems/pulling-on-leash.md`)
- [ ] One calm visitor, dog on leash or behind a gate, treats tossed on the floor
- [ ] Notice any problem pattern that has appeared (guarding, barking at the window, fear of something) and read the relevant problem page early
- [ ] Baseline numbers recorded for anything you want to change (`measurement-sheet.md`)
- [ ] Review the household plan together: is everyone using the same words?

## Signs the dog is settling

- Deep sleep in open rooms, on its side
- Play, including with you
- Seeking you out
- Eating normally and eliminating on schedule

## Signs to read more

- Not eating after several days → vet
- Panic when alone → `guide/03-problems/separation-anxiety.md`
- Stiffening over food or items → `guide/03-problems/resource-guarding.md`
- Hiding or trembling at ordinary things → `guide/03-problems/fear-and-noise-phobia.md`
- Any growl or snap at a household member → `guide/03-problems/aggression.md`
