# Household plan: one set of rules, one set of words

**Evidence for this page:** Moderate. Studies link owner inconsistency to lower obedience, and owner stress to harsher methods.
**Why it matters:** a dog cannot learn a rule that changes depending on who is home. Most "my dog listens to me but not my partner" problems are inconsistency, not disrespect.

## How to use

Print or copy this page, fill it in as a household, and put it on the fridge. Review it when something is not working.

## The one rule everyone agrees on

Nobody punishes the dog. Not shouting, not pushing, not pinning. If the dog did something wrong, we ask: what did it get out of it, and how do we prevent it next time?

## Children

Children follow the same words and rules, with an adult present for anything involving food, toys, or the dog's bed. Children do not wake a sleeping dog, hug it, or take things from it. Those are the top three bite situations.
