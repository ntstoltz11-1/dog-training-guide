# Pulling on the leash

**Evidence for this page:** Consensus for the method; Thin but concerning case evidence on neck injuries from tightening collars.
**See a vet first?** If pulling is new, or the dog pulls to one side or seems frantic. Also any coughing or gagging on the leash.

## What it is

The dog pulls because pulling works: it gets the dog where it wants to go. Every step you take while the leash is tight teaches the dog that pulling moves you.

## Before you start

- Switch to a **front-clip harness**. It turns the dog back toward you when it pulls and protects the neck. Fit guide in [`guide/01-start-here/equipment.md`](../01-start-here/equipment.md).
- Give the dog a real outlet for sniffing and exploring on a long line in a field, so walks are not the only chance to move.
- Pick a position word ("with me") and a side.

## Do this

1. At home with no distractions, reward the dog every time it is at your side with a loose leash. Feed at your leg so the position itself becomes the place good things happen.
2. On walks: the moment the leash tightens, **stop**. Stand still. When the dog turns back or the leash loosens, mark, feed at your side, and walk on. You are the tree that moves only for slack.
3. Add "penalty yards": if the dog pulls hard toward something, calmly walk a few steps backward before trying again. Pulling moves the goal further away.
4. Use what the dog wants as the paycheck: when the leash is loose for a few steps, say "go sniff" and let it. Sniffing becomes the reward for walking nicely.
5. Reward often at first (every few steps), then less, then unpredictably.

Starting numbers: short training walks of 10 minutes where the only goal is the leash, separate from exercise walks. Adjust to your dog.

## Avoid this

- **Choke chains, prong collars, leash jerks**: they work by pain. A case report describes brain injury after a choke-chain "hanging" correction. They are not shown to beat reward methods in any controlled comparison.
- **Retractable leashes**: the leash is always tight, which teaches pulling.
- **Walking on while the dog pulls "just this once"**: one win keeps the habit alive for weeks.

## How long it takes

One to three months for a reliable loose leash in normal places. Hard distractions (squirrels, other dogs) take longer and overlap with [`leash-reactivity.md`](leash-reactivity.md).

## Get help if

- The dog is too strong for you to hold safely
- Pulling comes with lunging at triggers
