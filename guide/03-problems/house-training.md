# House training

**Evidence for this page:** Consensus. The method is standard and uncontroversial; the one firm rule (no punishment) rests on how dogs learn.
**See a vet first?** Yes for any adult dog that was reliable and regressed. Bladder infections and other medical causes are common. Puppies: at the regular check-ups.

## What it is

Teaching the dog where to eliminate. The dog is not being stubborn or spiteful. It has not yet learned that outside is the place, and young puppies cannot hold it for long.

## Before you start

- A rough rule for puppies: they can hold it for about their age in months plus one, in hours, during the day. A 3-month-old needs a chance every 3 to 4 hours, and more often when active.
- Buy an enzymatic cleaner. Ordinary cleaners leave a scent that says "toilet here".
- Pick a spot outside and a word ("go potty").

## Do this

1. Take the dog out at every predictable moment: on waking, after eating or drinking, after play, before bed, and on a timer in between.
2. Go with the dog. Stand still and boring at the spot. Say the word once as the dog starts.
3. The moment the dog finishes, mark ("yes") and give a great treat **outside**, right there. Then play or walk. If the fun only starts after elimination, the dog learns to go quickly.
4. Between outings, the dog is supervised or confined. A puppy that disappears round a corner is about to have an accident. Use a leash tied to your belt, a pen, or a crate for short periods.
5. If you catch the dog mid-accident, interrupt gently ("oops, outside!"), carry or lead it out, and reward if it finishes there. If you find a puddle later, say nothing and clean it. The dog cannot connect it to anything.
6. Keep a simple log of accidents and successes for two weeks. Patterns appear fast: the time of day, the room, what happened just before.

## Avoid this

- **Rubbing the dog's nose in it or scolding**: the dog learns to hide when it eliminates, including from you outside, and may stop going in front of you.
- **Pee pads long-term** unless you want an indoor toilet: they teach "inside is fine".
- **Free run of the house too soon**: every unseen accident is a lesson in the wrong direction.

## How long it takes

Most puppies are reliable by about 4 to 6 months, with occasional slips until a year. Adult rescue dogs often learn in days to weeks once the routine is clear. A sudden regression in a trained adult is a vet visit, not a training problem.

## Get help if

- An adult dog regresses (vet first)
- A puppy over 6 months with no progress despite a consistent routine
- The dog eliminates only when alone (possible separation issue)
