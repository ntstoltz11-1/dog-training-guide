# Destructive chewing

**Evidence for this page:** Consensus.
**See a vet first?** Yes if the chewing is new in an adult dog or focused on the dog's own body. Teething, dental pain, and gut discomfort all drive chewing.

## What it is

Chewing is normal; dogs explore and self-soothe with their mouths. It becomes a problem when the target is your furniture, shoes, or walls. Three common drivers: teething (puppies under about 7 months), under-stimulation (a bored dog finds a project), and anxiety (chewing around doors and windows while you are out is a separation sign, not a chewing problem).

## Before you start

- Video the dog when it is alone. Chewing that starts within minutes of your leaving, aimed at exits, is [`separation-anxiety.md`](separation-anxiety.md).
- Dog-proof. Shoes in a closet, cords covered, a gate on the room with the good furniture. Every unsupervised chew of the sofa is a rewarding lesson.
- Buy legal chews of several textures: rubber toys that hold food, edible chews, a frozen stuffed toy for teething puppies.

## Do this

1. Rotate chews so a "new" one appears every few days.
2. When the dog chews something allowed, calmly praise. When it goes for something not allowed, interrupt gently and **trade** for a legal chew. Then praise the chew.
3. Add daily enrichment: a sniffing walk, a food puzzle, scattered kibble in grass. A tired mind chews less than a tired body.
4. Supervise or confine until the habit shifts. A dog that has not chewed furniture for a month earns more freedom.

## Avoid this

- **Punishing after the fact**: the dog cannot connect your anger to a shoe chewed an hour ago. It learns you are unpredictable.
- **Chasing to get the item back**: it becomes a great game. Trade instead.
- **Old shoes as toys**: the dog cannot tell old from new.

## How long it takes

Puppies grow out of teething by about 7 months; the habit of chewing allowed items takes weeks of consistency. Boredom chewing stops when the dog's day changes.

## Get help if

- Chewing is aimed at doors, windows, or crates while alone
- The dog swallows non-food items (a vet emergency)
