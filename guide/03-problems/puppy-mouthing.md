# Puppy mouthing and biting

**Evidence for this page:** Consensus. Normal development; the methods are standard.
**See a vet first?** Not usually. Sudden hard biting in a puppy that was gentle can mean teething pain; mention it at the next check.

## What it is

Puppies bite. It is how they play, explore, and learn how hard is too hard. Needle teeth hurt, but mouthing in a puppy under about 5 to 6 months is normal and declines with age. The job is to teach a soft mouth and to redirect the biting onto toys.

## Before you start

- **Sleep.** An overtired puppy bites more. Puppies need 16 to 20 hours a day. If biting spikes in the evening, the puppy needs a nap, not training.
- Have a toy within reach in every room.
- Teach everyone, especially children, the plan below. Children should not be left to manage a mouthy puppy alone.

## Do this

1. **Redirect.** When teeth find skin, offer a toy and make the toy exciting. Teeth on toy earns play.
2. **End the fun for hard bites.** If a bite hurts, say "ouch" calmly, stand up, and disengage for 10 to 20 seconds. Then return and offer a toy. The puppy learns that hard bites make the playmate leave.
3. **Reward gentle.** Mouthing that is soft can be allowed early on, then gradually expect softer, then no teeth. A puppy that learns to control bite force before its adult teeth arrive is safer for life.
4. **Teach an off switch.** Capture calm moments with a treat. Teach "sit" and "touch" so the puppy has something to do with its mouth closed.
5. **Manage the witching hour.** Evening zoomies and biting mean the puppy is overtired. Crate or pen with a chew.

Starting numbers: a 10 to 20 second pause after a hard bite; naps every 1 to 2 hours of waking for young puppies. Adjust to your puppy.

## Avoid this

- **Holding the muzzle shut, pushing the puppy over, or tapping the nose**: these teach the puppy that hands are a threat, and in clinic data, confrontational handling was linked to aggression later.
- **Rough wrestling with hands**: it teaches that hands are toys.
- **Yelping loudly**: some puppies get more excited. A calm pause works better.

## How long it takes

Mouthing fades steadily and is mostly gone by about 6 months with consistent handling. Biting that continues hard past that, or that breaks skin, is not normal puppy behavior.

## Get help if

- Bites break skin after 5 to 6 months of age
- Biting comes with stiffness, growling, or guarding rather than play
