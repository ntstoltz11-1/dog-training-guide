# Excessive barking

**Evidence for this page:** Consensus for the plan; Consensus (a European veterinary behavior position statement) against bark collars.
**See a vet first?** If barking is new, happens at night, or comes with pacing or confusion. Pain, hearing loss, and cognitive decline all cause barking.

## What it is

Barking has a job. Find the job and you find the fix:

| Type | What it looks like | What it gets the dog |
|---|---|---|
| **Alert / alarm** | At the window, door, or sounds outside | The thing goes away (it always does eventually) |
| **Demand** | At you, for food, play, or attention | You respond |
| **Fear** | At strangers, dogs, sounds, with stiff body or retreating | Distance |
| **Boredom / frustration** | Alone in the yard, at nothing in particular | Something to do |
| **Separation** | Starts within minutes of you leaving | See `separation-anxiety.md` |

## Before you start

- Video the barking. The trigger and the body language tell you which type it is.
- Cut the easy triggers: window film or closed curtains for alert barkers; no yard time alone for boredom barkers.

## Do this

**Alert barking**
1. Thank the dog ("got it") the first time it barks, call it to you, and reward. The dog learns one bark does the job.
2. Pair the trigger with food: a dog walks past, you drop treats on the floor. The passer-by starts to predict chicken instead of a threat. Card: `guide/04-protocols/counterconditioning-and-desensitization.md`.

**Demand barking**
1. Barking at you gets nothing: no eye contact, no words. Turn away.
2. Reward the dog heavily for quiet, polite ways of asking: sitting, lying down, bringing a toy. Give it the thing it wants only for those.
3. Expect a spike for a few days. Hold firm.

**Fear barking**: treat as fear, not noise. `fear-and-noise-phobia.md` and `leash-reactivity.md`.

**Boredom barking**: more enrichment, food puzzles, sniffing walks, and company. The barking is a symptom.

## Avoid this

- **Citronella, spray, or shock bark collars.** The European College of veterinary behaviorists states there is no credible evidence to justify them. They punish the symptom and can increase fear.
- **Shouting**: to the dog, you are joining in.
- **Yard time alone as "exercise"** for a barker: it is practice.

## How long it takes

Alert and demand barking: weeks. Fear barking: months, as for any fear. The extinction spike in demand barking lasts days if nobody gives in.

## Get help if

- Barking is paired with lunging or snapping
- Barking at night in an older dog (possible cognitive decline; see `guide/05-development/senior-dogs.md`)
- Neighbors are complaining and you need a fast management plan
