# Legal status of training tools

Legal facts carry a confidence level, not an evidence grade. **High** requires the statute or an official government page. Every row has a last-checked date. Check before relying on any row; laws change.

## Shock / electronic / remote collars

| Jurisdiction | Status | Confidence | Source | Last checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wales | Use banned: Animal Welfare (Electronic Collars) (Wales) Regulations 2010, SI 2010/943 (W.97), in force 2010-03-24. Regulation 2 prohibits attaching an electronic collar to a cat or dog. | High (statute read on legislation.gov.uk) | S-41 | 2026-10-08 |
| England | **Not banned.** Animal Welfare (Electronic Collars) (England) Regulations 2023 were drafted for 2024-02-01 but did not pass before Parliament ran out of time. DEFRA written answer 2026-01-14: the Government "will consult on whether to ban" later in this Parliament. Note: a Parliament petitions page (2025-03) wrongly states a ban took effect; the ministerial answer is more recent and authoritative. | High (ministerial answer plus Kennel Club policy page) | S-42, S-45, S-46 | 2026-10-08 |
| Scotland | Not banned. 2018 ministerial pledge to ban via guidance; later confirmed not prohibited. Amendment to ban voted down 84 to 28 on 2025-01-23 in the Welfare of Dogs (Scotland) Bill. | Moderate (news reporting) | S-43 | 2026-10-08 |
| Northern Ireland | Not banned | Moderate | secondary | 2026-10-08 |
| Germany | **Use banned.** Tierschutzgesetz section 3 no. 11; the Federal Administrative Court (3 C 14/05, 2006-02-23) held electric training devices in dog training prohibited with no exception for trained handlers. | High (statute plus highest court; state government page agrees) | S-60 | 2026-10-08 |
| Austria | **Use banned.** Tierschutzgesetz section 5(2)(3)(a): electrifying training devices prohibited; upheld against a constitutional challenge (VfGH G220/06, 2007). | High (statute via court record) | S-61 | 2026-10-08 |
| Netherlands | **Use banned on dogs** from 2022-01-01: Decree of 2021-07-14 (Stb. 2021, 361) added to article 1.3 of the Besluit houders van dieren the use of equipment able to give a dog electric shocks; exceptions for professional veterinary use, police, military police, armed forces, and electric fencing. | High (Staatsblad text) | S-62 | 2026-10-08 |
| Switzerland | **Use banned.** Animal Welfare Ordinance (TSchV, SR 455.1) art. 76(2): devices that electrify, emit very unpleasant acoustic signals, or act by chemicals may not be used on dogs; art. 76(3) lets a cantonal authority permit therapeutic use by a trained person as an exception. | High (federal BLV fact sheet; cantonal permit form) | S-63 | 2026-10-08 |
| Denmark | **Use banned.** Order 815 of 2023 on certain devices and collars for animals, section 2: use of any remote-controlled or automatically acting device that gives an animal an electric shock is prohibited (earlier Order of 2009; reported in force since 2000). Prong collars banned under the dog-keeping Order (BEK 607 of 2025-05-26). | High (Retsinformation) | S-64 | 2026-10-08 |
| Sweden | **Use banned.** Animal Welfare Ordinance (2019:66) ch. 2 s. 16: equipment that gives an animal an electric shock in order to control its behaviour may not be used or installed. A 2025 amendment (SFS 2025:948) regulates GPS "virtual fence" collars for grazing livestock; nothing found changing the dog position. | High (Riksdag statute text) | S-65 | 2026-10-08 |
| Norway | **Use banned** with a narrow exception: Regulation of 2008-03-14 no. 256 on the use of electric current in dog training bans all shock equipment; aversion training against chasing livestock, reindeer, and cloven-hoofed game is allowed only by persons meeting competence requirements; service-dog work also excepted. Illegal use carries an administrative fine. | High (Mattilsynet, the government inspectorate) | S-66 | 2026-10-08 |
| Finland | Reported ban. New Animal Welfare Act (693/2023) in force 2024-01-01; the specific section on electric devices was not located this pass. | Moderate | secondary; Act located (Finlex) | 2026-10-08 |
| Jersey (Channel Islands) | Animal Welfare (Jersey) Law 2026 passed 2026-03; consultation (closed 2026-07-14) on prohibiting electronic, pinch, and prong collars under it | Moderate (government consultation document) | gov.je consultation letter | 2026-10-08 |
| France | National Assembly voted for a ban in early 2023; implementation status unverified | Low | secondary | 2026-10-08 |
| Australia (by state) | Reported restrictions in some states (for example NSW, SA, ACT) | Low | not researched | 2026-10-08 |
| Quebec, Canada | Reported restrictions | Low | not researched | 2026-10-08 |
| United States | No federal restriction; check state and municipal law | Moderate | general knowledge | 2026-10-08 |

## Bark collars (spray, sound, shock)

Covered by the shock-collar rows where shock is involved. Spray and sound collars are generally legal; ESVCE (2018) advises against all electronic training devices on evidence grounds (S-28).

## Dominance techniques

Hitting, kicking, and hanging by the collar may constitute offenses under general animal welfare law in most jurisdictions. Not researched in detail.
