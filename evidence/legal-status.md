# Legal status of training tools

Legal facts carry a confidence level, not an evidence grade. **High** requires the statute or an official government page. Every row has a last-checked date. Check before relying on any row; laws change.

## Bark collars (spray, sound, shock)

Covered by the shock-collar rows where shock is involved. Spray and sound collars are generally legal; ESVCE (2018) advises against all electronic training devices on evidence grounds (S-28).

## Dominance techniques

Hitting, kicking, and hanging by the collar may constitute offenses under general animal welfare law in most jurisdictions. Not researched in detail.
