# Comparison matrices

Current answer only. Reader-facing versions live in `guide/02-methods/overview-and-comparison.md`. Edit here first, then the guide page.

## Methods

| Attribute | Reward-based | LIMA / Humane Hierarchy | Balanced | Compulsion / dominance | Relationship / CU / BAT / DAID | ABA framing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Learning basis | R+, P-, extinction, classical CC | Ordered use of all quadrants, least intrusive first | R+ plus P+ and R- | P+ and R- primarily | R+, classical CC, choice, social learning; R- by distance (BAT 1.0) | All operant and respondent processes |
| Evidence base | Largest; consistent; mainly correlational plus 2 controlled field studies (C-01, C-02, C-04) | Inherits R+ evidence; ethical framework (C-05) | Not superior; welfare costs (C-01, C-02, C-27) | High aggression risk (C-03); rationale retracted (C-06) | Thin for CU and BAT; Moderate for DAID tasks (C-22, C-30) | Strong in general behavior science; few dog trials |
| Acquisition speed | Equal or faster | Same as R+ | No advantage shown | No advantage shown | Not measured | Depends on procedure |
| Reliability / generalization | Good if proofed (3 Ds) | Good | Claimed; not demonstrated vs R+ | Suppression may not generalize; context fear does | Good for arousal regulation (practitioner reports) | Good with explicit generalization plans |
| Welfare risk | Low | Low | Moderate to high, dose-dependent | High | Low | Low if R+ led |
| Handler skill | Moderate | Moderate to high (judgment) | High for safe use | Low to apply, high risk | Moderate to high (threshold reading) | High |
| Legal issues | None | None | E-collars banned in Wales and several European countries (C-13) | Some techniques may breach welfare law | None | None |
| Best fit | Default for all dogs | Professional decision rules | Not recommended by AVSAB, ESVCE, BVA | Not recommended | Reactivity, fear, overarousal | Case analysis, measurement |
| Failure modes | Bribery, lure dependence, low rate of reinforcement | "Needed" aversives creep | Escalation, fear fallout, suppressed warnings | Aggression, shutdown | Working over threshold | Ignoring emotion and pain |
| Verdict | Default | Recommended framework | Described, not recommended | Not recommended | Useful | Measuring backbone |

## Behavior problems

| Problem | Primary protocol | Evidence | Typical timeline | Guide page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leash reactivity | Management + CC/DS + LAT or engage-disengage + BAT | Consensus (thin trials) | 3 to 12 months | 03/leash-reactivity |
| Noise phobia | Safe haven + CC/DS + situational medication | Strong (medication); Consensus (CC/DS) | Immediate (medication); months (CC/DS) | 03/fear-and-noise-phobia |
